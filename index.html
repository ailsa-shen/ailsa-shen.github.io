Ailsa Shen

Applied and Computational Mathematics, Caltech

I am a third-year undergraduate at the California Institute of Technology. My research focuses on mechanistic interpretability and representation learning. In particular, I am interested in understanding the internal representations of neural networks, why specific structures emerge, and how they shape model behavior. I’m also interested in the mathematical foundations of machine learning, as well as in understanding how artificial and biological intelligence intersect.

Currently, I am a member of Caltech’s AI + Science Lab, where I work on mechanistic interpretability for neural operators with Prof. Anima Anandkumar and Prof. Bahareh Tolooshams. I also work with the RyCo Lab at ETH Zurich in psycholinguistics and cognitive modelling, advised by Prof. Ryan Cotterell and Dr. Karolina Stańczak.

I am grateful to be supported by the Mellon Mays Undergraduate Fellowship for my research at Caltech.
